Now we encounter a very important production problem:

Just because an agent is authentic, does that mean it's trustworthy?

No.

Authentication proves:

You are who you claim to be.


It does not prove:

You are good at your job.
You are reliable.
You are accurate.
You are safe.


In large ANP ecosystems, trust and reputation become critical once you have many independently operated agents participating in the network. The ANP vision emphasizes interoperable cross-domain agent networks built on identity and discovery foundations.

Think:

1 catastrophic failure


Not all events have equal impact.

Production systems often weight failures more heavily.

Python Trust Router
def select_trusted_agent(
    agents,
    minimum_trust=80
):

    eligible = [

        a

        for a in agents

        if a["trust_score"]
           >= minimum_trust
    ]

    return max(

        eligible,

        key=lambda a:
        a["trust_score"]
    )


Usage:

best = select_trusted_agent(
    agents
)
